When “Just Tell Me What To Do” Still Leaves You Carrying Everything

The Handover Playbook is designed for people who are tired of being the person who remembers, plans, reminds, follows up, and makes sure everything actually gets done.

Instead of simply asking for help, this playbook focuses on creating clear ownership of recurring responsibilities.

What You’ll Learn

  • How to identify the responsibilities creating invisible mental load.
  • How to turn recurring tasks into clear ownership.
  • How to communicate a handover without taking the responsibility back.
  • How to create practical systems that make responsibilities easier to manage.
  • How to reduce the constant remembering, reminding, and follow-up cycle.

What’s Inside

The Handover Playbook is a practical digital resource built around real-world recurring responsibilities and the communication patterns that can make ownership unclear.

It gives readers a structured way to move from “helping” to genuine responsibility and clearer ownership.

Who It’s For

  • People who feel responsible for remembering everything.
  • Couples struggling with an uneven mental load.
  • People who repeatedly have to remind their partner about responsibilities.
  • Anyone looking for a practical framework for clearer ownership.
